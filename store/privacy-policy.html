Bedtime Stories — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Bedtime Stories is a story app that families use together at bedtime. Children are part of its audience, so it is built to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, and the app itself has no analytics and no servers.

What stays on your device

  • Your child's name and character. The name you type and the look you choose are saved only on the device, so the stories can use them. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Favourites, settings and stories you make. Saved only on the device.
  • Voice recordings. If a parent chooses "My voice" and records a page, the recording is saved only on the device. The microphone is used only while you are recording, and only after you allow it.

The app is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so this information is not copied to a Google account or to a new phone. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

How stories are read aloud

  • English is read by a voice built into the app. It runs entirely on the device and needs no internet connection.
  • Other languages are read by your phone's own text-to-speech service (on most phones, Speech Recognition & Synthesis from Google). The app hands that service the text of the page being read. Depending on the service and its settings, it may send that text over the internet to produce the speech. These stories do not contain your child's name.
  • If the built-in English voice cannot run on a device, the phone's text-to-speech service reads the English stories instead. In that case the page text it receives can include the child's name.

The phone's text-to-speech service is not part of this app and has its own privacy policy.

Advertising

The app shows one banner advertisement on its browsing screens (the home, story-maker and profile screens). No advertisement is shown while a story is being read, and the app never shows full-screen advertisements.

Advertisements are provided by Google AdMob. Because children use this app:

  • every advertisement request is marked as child-directed and as being for users under the age of consent, which turns off personalised advertising;
  • advertisements are limited to content rated for general audiences;
  • the app does not request the Android advertising ID.

To deliver and measure advertisements, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects some information automatically, such as the device's IP address (which can indicate a general location), basic device and app information, diagnostic data, and whether an advertisement was shown or tapped. Google's handling of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app uses the internet only to load this advertisement.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for an account, an email address or a phone number.
  • We do not collect, store or receive any personal information ourselves.
  • We do not sell information to anyone.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for families, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only third party that receives any data is the advertising provider described above, configured for child-directed treatment. A parent or guardian who has questions can contact us at the address below.

Permissions

  • Microphone — optional, used only when a parent records their own reading of a page.
  • Internet and network state — used to load the banner advertisement.
